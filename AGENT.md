# 🧑‍💼 ABUJA REALTY PLATFORM — AGENT GUIDE (agent.md)

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This document defines how real estate agents and internal staff interact with the Abuja Realty Platform system. It explains responsibilities, workflows, and operational rules for managing properties, leads, and content.

The platform is designed to help agents:

* Manage property listings efficiently
* Respond to client inquiries quickly
* Publish and maintain blog content
* Track leads and conversions

---

## 2. ROLE DEFINITIONS

### 2.1 Admin

* Full system access
* Manages users, agents, and settings
* Oversees all listings and content

### 2.2 Agent

* Manages assigned property listings
* Responds to leads and inquiries
* Updates property status (available, sold, pending)

### 2.3 Content Editor (optional role)

* Creates and edits blog posts
* Optimizes content for SEO

---

## 3. AGENT RESPONSIBILITIES

### Property Management

* Add new property listings with full details
* Upload high-quality images and media
* Ensure accurate pricing and location data
* Update property status regularly

### Lead Management

* Respond to inquiries within 24 hours
* Track and update lead status
* Follow up with potential buyers/renters

### Client Communication

* Handle WhatsApp, email, and phone inquiries
* Schedule property inspections
* Provide accurate and professional responses

---

## 4. PROPERTY LISTING RULES

Every listing must include:

* Clear title (e.g., 4 Bedroom Duplex in Maitama)
* Accurate price (no placeholders)
* Verified location (Abuja districts only)
* Minimum 5 high-quality images
* Detailed description (features + benefits)
* Property type (rent, sale, land)
* Status (Available / Sold / Pending)

---

## 5. LEAD HANDLING WORKFLOW

1. New lead arrives via contact form or WhatsApp
2. Agent receives notification
3. Agent reviews property inquiry details
4. Respond within 24 hours
5. Schedule inspection if needed
6. Update lead status:

   * New
   * Contacted
   * Inspection Scheduled
   * Converted
   * Closed

---

## 6. BLOG CONTENT GUIDELINES

Agents or editors contributing to blogs must:

* Focus on Abuja real estate market
* Use SEO-friendly titles
* Include keywords such as:

  * “houses in Abuja”
  * “buy land in Abuja”
* Keep content informative and trustworthy
* Avoid misleading investment claims

---

## 7. MEDIA STANDARDS

### Images

* Minimum resolution: 1200px width
* No blurry or low-quality images
* Proper lighting and framing required

### Videos (if used)

* Maximum 2–3 minutes walkthrough
* Stable, professional recording

---

## 8. COMMUNICATION STANDARDS

Agents must:

* Be professional and polite at all times
* Avoid overpromising property availability
* Provide accurate and verified information only
* Respond quickly to maintain lead conversion rates

---

## 9. SECURITY RULES

* Do not share admin credentials
* Do not publish fake or unverified listings
* Do not modify system settings without permission
* Ensure data privacy of clients is respected

---

## 10. PERFORMANCE EXPECTATIONS

Agents are evaluated based on:

* Response time to leads
* Number of successful conversions
* Accuracy of listings
* Client satisfaction feedback

---

## 11. SYSTEM TOOLS USAGE

Agents can use the dashboard to:

* Add/Edit/Delete properties
* Manage blog posts (if authorized)
* Track leads and conversions
* View analytics reports

---

## 12. CONCLUSION

This agent guide ensures consistency, professionalism, and efficiency in managing real estate operations on the Abuja Realty Platform. Proper adherence improves trust, lead conversion, and overall system performance.
